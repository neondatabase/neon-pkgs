After installing the Neon plugin for Cursor, `neon plugins`, `neon init`, and `neon bootstrap` now say that Cursor loads it from `~/.claude/plugins` only when "Include Third-Party Plugins, Skills, and Other Configs" is on in Cursor Settings > Agents > Third-Party Imports, and that `/add-plugin neon-postgres` in Cursor chat is the alternative.
