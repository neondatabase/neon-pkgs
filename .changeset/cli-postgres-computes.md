Add `neon postgres` (aliases `pg` and `lakebase`) with `computes`, `databases`, `roles`, `connection-string`, and `psql`. `neon postgres computes` lists, gets, updates, starts, suspends, restarts, and deletes computes, and waits for each change to be applied unless you pass `--no-wait`. The top-level `roles`, `databases`, `connection-string`, and `psql` commands are unchanged.
