Add Neon incident reporting from neonstatus.com: `listNeonIncidents()` and `neon.incidents.list()` in `@neon/sdk` (also on the `@neon/effect` client), the `list_incidents` tool (`incidents.list`) in `@neon/tools`, and the `neon incidents` command. None of them need a Neon credential.
